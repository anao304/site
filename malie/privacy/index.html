Mālie Privacy Policy

Mālie is designed so you can move forward in complete privacy. No account, no name, no email: we don’t need to know who you are to help you. This page explains, in plain words, what is kept, where, and why.

Who is responsible for your data

The Mālie app is published by Rising Labs (SASU), 61 rue de Lyon, 75012 Paris. For any question about your data: contact@rising-labs.app.

What stays only on your phone

As long as you don’t talk to Lou, none of this leaves your phone:

  • your answers to the opening test and your starting point (drinks per week, average price of a drink);
  • your goal, your why and your plan (your line, your outlets, your go-to drinks, your “if… then…” plans, what you write in the program cards);
  • the drinks you log, your alcohol-free days, your milestones, your calendar, your nights out and the cravings you’ve beaten;
  • the history of your conversations with Lou.

We have no access to it. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. If you write to Lou, part of this information is sent to her so she can answer you: the details are just below.

What is sent when you talk to Lou

To answer you, Lou uses an artificial intelligence service. When you write to her, we send:

  • your latest messages in the conversation;
  • a summary of your journey so she can answer you personally: your goal, your why, your alcohol-free days and your streak, your drinks this week and your weekly goal, your starting point, the money kept and the calories skipped, your next milestone, the cravings you’ve beaten and your alcohol-free nights out, your risky moments, your plans, your line, your outlets, your go-to drinks, today’s program card and your planned night out;
  • your local time, the language of your phone and of the app, and the country set on your iPhone, so she answers in your language;
  • a random identifier created on your phone, which contains neither your name, nor your phone number, nor your email address;
  • your proof of purchase signed by Apple (transaction identifier, product purchased, dates), to check that your access to Mālie is active. It contains neither your name, nor your email, nor your bank card.

These messages may contain information about your health. That is why we ask for your consent before the first one is sent. You can keep using everything else in Mālie without talking to Lou.

This data goes through two service providers:

  • Supabase, which hosts our small server in Europe. It checks your proof of purchase and keeps only the number of messages sent per day, tied to a fingerprint of your purchase (a code that cannot be used to trace the purchase) or to your random identifier, along with the first 8 characters of that identifier. These counters are used to apply message limits (45 per day; 10 per day for a while beyond 500 messages in 30 days) and are erased after 30 days. The content of your messages is not stored there.
  • Anthropic, the company that provides Lou’s artificial intelligence, located in the United States. It receives your messages and the information listed above, except your random identifier and your proof of purchase. It processes your messages to generate the answer, does not use them to train its models and keeps them only for a limited time (30 days at most as a general rule), unless required by law or if content is flagged for safety reasons. This transfer outside the European Union is covered by safeguards provided for by the GDPR, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Lou is an artificial intelligence: she is neither a doctor nor a psychologist. If you are in danger, contact the emergency services in your country.

Payment and subscription

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your bank card, your name or your Apple ID. When you write to Lou, the app sends our server your proof of purchase signed by Apple, only to check that your access is active. To manage or cancel your subscription: iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

Face ID and notifications

Face ID lock is handled by your iPhone: Mālie never sees your face or your passcode. Reminders (card of the day, nights out, milestones) are scheduled directly on your phone, without going through our servers.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no selling of data, no advertising trackers.
  • No statistics on how you use the app.
  • No account, no email, no name requested.

Why we process this data

  • Conversations with Lou are based on your explicit consent, which you give before the first message and can withdraw at any time by no longer writing to her and deleting the conversation.
  • The daily message limit and the check of your proof of purchase are based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service against abuse.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing or object to it, by writing to contact@rising-labs.app. Since we don’t know your identity, you will need to give us the anonymous identifier shown at the bottom of this page in the app, so we can find your counters. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here. If the change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
